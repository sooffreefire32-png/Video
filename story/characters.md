# 👥 Character Sheets — KalKatha Toons

Flat 2D cartoon designs (Chroma Toons style). All assets are generated as original vector art in `public/assets/` by `tools/gen-assets.js` — no copyrighted images.

---

## 1. Raghu (राघव) — The Hero · 22
- **Look:** Blue kurta + worn jeans, messy black hair, one stray curl, worn white sneakers.
- **Expressions:** Worried (default) → Wide-eyed shock → Greedy grin → Tears of relief.
- **Body language:** Hands in pockets when broke; fast walker; on the run he clutches a cloth bag.
- **Colors:** Kurta `#1d4ed8`, jeans `#1e3a5f`, skin `#e8b48c`, hair `#201a17`.

## 2. Amma / Bimla — The Mother
- **Look:** Orange saree with gold border, grey hair in a bun, thin shawl.
- **Always** has a small steel glass of water / cough. Weak but proud smile.
- **Colors:** Saree `#ea580c`, border `#fbbf24`, shawl `#a8a29e`.

## 3. Kalu Chacha — The Shopkeeper
- **Look:** White kurta, round glasses, grey moustache, cotton gamcha on shoulder.
- **Sage of the story** — his line *"kal wala paisa kisi ke kaam ka nahi hota"* is the hidden moral.
- **Colors:** Kurta `#f5f5f4`, skin `#c98a5a`, glasses silver.

## 4. Inspector Sher Singh — The Cop
- **Look:** Khaki uniform, black belt, big moustache, wooden lathi, filmi attitude.
- **Colors:** Uniform `#a16207`, moustache `#292524`, cap khaki.

## 5. Kavya (काव्या) — Time-Police, 2087
- **Look:** White-silver futuristic coat, cyan visor/eyes, glowing oval badge (TIME POLICE).
- **Moves weightless**; calm voice; the one who brings the twist.
- **Colors:** Coat `#e2e8f0` with silver sheen, visor `#22d3ee`, badge glow `#a5f3fc`.

## 6. The ATM — "MahaLakshmi Bank · Branch 13"
- **Look:** Red-brick booth, peeling blue signboard, no visible wires, screen **always 12:00 AM**.
- **Screen states:** `INSERT CARD` → amber clock counting upward → `TRANSACTION FROM TOMORROW` (red) → city of 2087 → golden dust.
- **Voice:** warm + ancient + electric.

---

## 🎭 Expression packs used in render
The renderer applies caption text + mood color-grading per scene. Character poses are baked into scene SVGs (Raghu worried / shocked / grinning / crying; Kavya calm; Inspector furious).
