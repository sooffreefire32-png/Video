## 🎬 FULL SCRIPT (Timestamps = narration start times)

### SCENE 1 — HOOK (00:00 – 00:45) ⚡

**[VISUAL]** Black screen. Sound of heavy rain. A single ATM booth glows at the end of a dark empty street. Camera pushes in slowly. Inside, a trembling hand puts in a card.

**NARRATOR (deep, dramatic):**
> *"Aaj ki raat, ek ladke ne ATM se paise nikale. Lekin jab usne note ko haath mein uthaya… toh note par koi aur hi date chhapi hui thi."*

**[SFX]** Machine whirr → notes sliding out → **THUNDER.**

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Kal ki date. Aur woh paisa… kaam kar raha tha. Bilkul asli ki tarah. Lekin jo baat aage hone wali thi, usne is shehar ka waqt hi badal ke rakh diya."*

**[VISUAL]** On the ATM screen, one line in glowing red: **"TRANSACTION FROM TOMORROW"** — then static.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Yeh hai… Kal Wale ATM ki kahani."*

---

### SCENE 2 — RAGHU KI ZINDAGI (00:45 – 02:00) 🏚️

**[VISUAL]** Morning. A small rented room, peeling walls, one tube light flickering. Raghu checks his phone — no balance alert, just bills. Amma's cough from the other room.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Is kahani ka hero, Raghu. B.Com ka aakhri saal. Din mein delivery karne wala, raat ko padhai. Ghar mein do kamre, ek dawai, aur sau sapne."*

**[VISUAL]** Kalu Chacha's shop. Raghu asks for a loan. Chacha shakes his head slowly, counting coins into his palm.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Uske paas sab kuch tha… except paisa. Aur jab uski Amma ki tabiyat kharab ho gayi, doctor ne jo dawai likhi, uska bill dekh kar Raghu ki aankhon ke aage andhera aa gaya. Aath hazaar rupaye. Uske paas… sirf do sau the."*

**AMMA (soft, from bed):**
> *"Beta… tension mat le. Purani dawai se kaam chal jayega."*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Lekin Raghu jaanta tha — purani dawai se kaam nahi chalega. Aur ussi raat, jab sab so rahe the, woh nikal pada. Pahadi Road ke us adde par… jahan woh bataata tha ki ek purana ATM hai. Jo kisi network se connect nahi hota. Jo kabhi band nahi hota."*

---

### SCENE 3 — THE ATM (02:00 – 03:00) 🏧

**[VISUAL]** 12:10 AM. Raghu stands before the rusty ATM booth. Red bricks, peeling "MAHALAKSHMI BANK" sign. Screen glows: **"INSERT CARD"** — even though the machine has no wire visible.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Machine purani thi. Na wiring thi, na koi shop. Lekin screen par light thi. Raghu ne socha — 'Ek baar try karke dekhta hoon. Kya hi hoga?'"*

**[SFX]** Card inserted. Deep hum. The screen turns amber, then shows numbers that **weren't his balance** — a clock counting **upward**.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Usne 5000 rupaye nikale. Machine ne karak kar ke note diye. Aur phir screen par kuch aisa aaya jo Raghu ko hasa diya — shayad woh aankhon se dhoka tha. Note par likha tha: **KAL KI DATE.**"*

**[VISUAL]** Close-up: crisp ₹500 note. In the corner, printed: **01-01-2087.** Raghu blinks. Blinks again. Stuffs it in his pocket and runs.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Kal ki date wala paisa. Raghu ne socha — koi printing mistake hogi. Lekin woh galat tha. Yeh koi mistake nahi thi. Yeh… invitation tha."*

---

### SCENE 4 — PAISA JO KAAM KARTA HAI (03:00 – 04:15) 💸

**[VISUAL]** Pharmacy. Raghu hands over the note. Cashier scans, counts, gives change — **no alarm**. Amma gets her medicines. Day turns to week.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Sabse bada twist yeh tha — **paisa kaam kar raha tha.** Pharmacy ne accept kiya. Kirana wale ne accept kiya. Auto wale ne bhi."*

**[VISUAL]** Montage: Raghu buys a new shirt, pays the landlord, treats Amma to halwa. He's smiling. Then he returns to the ATM. Twice. Five times. Notes pile up in a drawer.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Ek hafte mein Raghu ki zindagi badal gayi. Kalu Chacha ke store par, usne paisa gira diya. Lekin chacha ne note uthaya… aur ruk gaye. Ungli se date ko chhua. **'Beta… yeh note kal ka hai?'**"*

**KALU CHACHA (slow, uneasy):**
> *"Bachcha, paisa wahi hota hai jo aaj chalta hai. Kal wala paisa… kisi ke kaam ka nahi hota."*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Raghu ne hasi mein baat taal di. Lekin raat ko jab TV par aaya — *'RBI ne naye note jari kiye hain, jinki date aane wale kal ki hai'* — toh usne TV band kar diya. Kyonki woh jaanta tha. Woh notes… uske drawer mein the."*

---

### SCENE 5 — MIDNIGHT TWIST (04:15 – 05:45) 🚨

**[VISUAL]** 12:00 AM sharp. Every phone in the city pings. Breaking news ticker: **"BREAKING: NOTES WITH TOMORROW'S DATE DECLARED ILLEGAL — TIME-STAMPED COUNTERFEIT."** Sirens. Headlights at Raghu's door.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Aur phir aadhi raat ko… woh hua jiski kisi ko ummeed nahi thi. Tees din ki date wale saare notes, ek second mein illegal ho gaye. Jinki date aaj hai, woh asli. Jinki date **kal** hai… woh ab jaalsaaz the."*

**[VISUAL]** Inspector Sher Singh kicks the door open. Raghu grabs the drawer and jumps out the back window — notes flying in the wind like dead leaves.

**INSPECTOR (thundering):**
> *"Raghu! Woh note jis pe kal ki date hai — woh note criminal hai! Ruk ja!"*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Raghu bhaaga. Jaeb mein woh paisa, jo raat ko asli tha aur ab faaltu. Ek sawaal uske saath bhaag raha tha — *agar yeh paisa jaalsaaz hai, toh us din pharmacy wale ne ise kyon accept kiya? Koi machine ise kaise sachcha maan li?*"*

---

### SCENE 6 — WAPSI ATM PAR (05:45 – 07:15) 🔍

**[VISUAL]** 3:00 AM. Rain. Raghu, drenched, reaches the ATM again. This time the machine is **humming louder**. The screen doesn't ask for a card. It shows a city — flying cars, glass towers, **2087**.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Teen baje, woh wapas wahan tha. Machine ne card nahi maanga. Screen par ek shehar dikha — hawa mein gaadiyan, sheeshe ke minaar, aur sabse bada number: **2087.**"*

**THE ATM (a voice — warm, ancient, electric):**
> *"Raghu… main koi bank nahi hoon. Main ek darpan hoon. Tu jo aaj dekh raha hai, woh mera shehar hai. Aur yeh paisa jo tune nikala… woh paisa nahi hai. Woh **waqt** hai."*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Raghu peeche hat gaya. Machine ne baat ki thi. Uski awaaz mein itni purani sadak thi ki dar lagta tha. Aur tabhi — screen ki roshni mein ek aur cheez aa gayi. Ek insaan. Silver coat. Cyan aankhein. Aur haath mein ek chhota sa card, jis par likha tha — **TIME POLICE.**"*

---

### SCENE 7 — KAVYA (07:15 – 08:45) 👮‍♀️

**[VISUAL]** A woman steps out of the light. She is calm, weightless. Behind her, the screen shows a timeline splitting into cracks.

**KAVYA:**
> *"Mera naam Kavya hai. Time-Police, 2087. Raghu, yeh ATM kisi aur ne nahi banaya. Yehm ATM banaya tha… **RAGHAV NE.**"*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Raghu hans diya. 'Raghav kaun hai? Bank ka manager?'"*

**KAVYA (softly):**
> *"Raghav… 2087 ka sabse ameer aadmi hai. Woh aadmi jo rocket nahi, duniya ka sabse bada bank chalaata hai — ek bank jo **kal se udhaar deta hai.** Aur woh aadmi… tu hai, Raghu."*

**[VISUAL]** Raghu's reflection in the screen. For a second, it's older. Wrinkled. Wearing a silver coat. It smiles at him. SFX: heartbeat.

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Pura shehar ruk gaya. Raghu ka dimaag kaam karna band ho gaya. Kavya ne kaha — saal 2087 mein, Raghav ne apni maa khoyi thi. Aur us dard se bachne ke liye usne ek ATM banaya — jo apne **bachepan** mein paisa bhejta hai. Taaki uska purana wala khud, apni Amma ko bacha sake. Lekin…"*

**KAVYA:**
> *"Lekin har note jo tu aaj kharach karega, wo kisi aur ka kal chura lega. Teri har khushi, kisi aur ki maa ka hissa. Time toot raha hai, Raghu. Meri job yeh ATM band karna hai. Aur tera sawaal hai — **wahi rahega jo woh raat tha: paisa… ya Amma.**"*

---

### SCENE 8 — THE CHOICE (08:45 – 09:30) 🔥

**[VISUAL]** Rain stops. Raghu opens the bag — the notes from the future, still crisp. The drawer of money. Then his phone rings — Amma's number. On the other end, her weak voice: *"Beta… dawai khatam ho gayi."*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Duniya bhar ka paisa, aur ek maa ki dawai. Raghu ke haath kaap rahe the. Woh soch sakta tha — ek aur mahina. Ek aur nikalta. Amma theek. Aur phir? Kal tootega. Woh khud mita jaayega. **Aur phir woh kahani jo usne banayi thi, kisi ki maa ko kha jayegi.**"*

**[VISUAL]** Slow-motion close-up on Raghu's face — tear. He looks at the machine. At the reflection of future-Raghav, whose eyes are pleading — *because future-Raghav also lost his mother.*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Aur tab Raghu ne woh kiya jo future ke sabse ameer aadmi ne kabhi nahi socha tha. Usne notes ko faad diya. Ek-ek karke. Kal ka paisa, aaj ki baarish mein."*

**RAGHU (gritting):**
> *"Kal mera nahi hai. Kal kisi aur ka hai. Mera aaj hai — aur aaj main apni Amma ko dawai de sakta hoon, apne paison se. **Apne asli paison se.**"*

---

### SCENE 9 — KAL KA INSAF (09:30 – 10:00) 🌅

**[VISUAL]** The notes burn and dissolve into light. The ATM trembles, crumbles into golden dust. Kavya watches — almost sad. As she fades into static, she puts one last thing in his pocket.

**KAVYA (smiling, fading):**
> *"Tumne aaj chuna, Raghu. Isliye tumhara kal aayega."*

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Subah. Raghu ne aankh kholi. Amma ki khansi… gayab thi. Kitchen se halwe ki khushbu aa rahi thi. Aur uski jaeb mein ek note tha. Sirf ek. Jis par date thi — **AAJ KI.** Aur uske neeche chhote se aksharon mein likha tha:"*

**[VISUAL]** Close-up of the note, signature in silver ink:
> **"Amma theek hai. — Tumhara Kal wala Khud"**

**NARRATOR:**
> *"Kavya gayi thi. ATM gayi thi. Lekin woh ek note raha. Kyonki kuch cheezein waqt se nahi badalti. Aur kal — kal koi jagah nahi hai jo milti hai. **Kal ek jagah hai jo banti hai.** Aur jo log aaj ke saath imaandaar hote hain… unka kal, khud unke paas chala aata hai."*

**[END CARD]** KalKatha Toons logo. Music swells. Text: **"AGLA SAWAL: AUR AGAR NOTE PAR PARSON KI DATE HOTI…?"**

**NARRATOR (teaser voice):**
> *"Aur haan — woh ATM jisne yeh kahani likhi, wo aaj bhi Pahadi Road par khada hai. Screen par ab bhi 12:00 AM hai. Bas… ab us par kisi aur ka card laga hua hai. Like, share, aur subscribe karna mat bhoolna. Kyonki kal… kabhi door nahi hota."*
