# 🧬 STATE 4 — Style DNA

> Reference channel: **Bob Invests** (from the video) — 86 subscribers, 38 videos, **$3.6M views, $10K+ earned**.
> Niche: behavioral personal finance. Audience: US working class. Language: English, American accent.

## How the channel works (not what it says — HOW it works)

| DNA element | Extraction |
|---|---|
| **Niche** | Behavioral personal finance — money decisions told through real people's stories |
| **Target audience** | Working middle-class adults (25–45) making everyday money choices: car loans, savings, credit |
| **Hook style** | Open mid-scene on a relatable character's dilemma — no intro, no channel branding. Example from the video: *"Marcus needed a car. He had $22,000 in savings and a 720 credit score."* |
| **Script flow** | Story → the *smart-sounding* decision → hidden cost reveal → the math → lesson + direct call to the viewer |
| **Sentence rhythm** | Short, punchy declarative sentences. 6–12 words average. Numbers dropped hard mid-sentence |
| **Tone** | Calm, matter-of-fact, slightly dramatic on reveals. Feels like a smart friend doing the math for you |
| **Transitions** | "But here's the thing…", "Let's run the numbers.", "Now —", "And that's not the worst part." |
| **Curiosity gaps** | Dealer "told him something that sounded smart" — you have to watch to learn what and why it's wrong |
| **Emotional triggers** | Fear of being taken advantage of · relief of clarity · pride of a smart decision |
| **Retention techniques** | Number stakes ($8,000 of interest), mini-cliffhangers, mid-video re-hook ("seven-year math"), direct address ("you") |
| **Direct address** | Heavy second-person: "you", "your savings", "your payment" |
| **Words per second** | ~2.5 (slow, deliberate voiceover, American accent) |
| **Average word count** | 2,000–2,300 words per full video (~13–15 min) |

## VISUAL DNA (from the video's channel walkthrough)

- **No live video** — pure **images + AI voiceover** (faceless channel)
- Every image has a **slow zoom** (in/out) effect — no static shots
- **Floating dust particles** overlay on every scene
- **Subtitles always on**, bottom-center
- Flat, clean **illustrated scenes** — warm cinematic color grade, soft light
- 16:9 landscape (1280×720+), each beat ≈ **3–5 seconds**
